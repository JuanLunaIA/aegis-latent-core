# Refund Policy

> **Draft for counsel review.** `[COUNSEL-REVIEW-REQUIRED]` This document is not legal advice and is not an offer; it is prepared for review by qualified counsel.


**Version:** 1.0-draft · **Status:** `[COUNSEL-REVIEW-REQUIRED]`

## 1. First purchase

A first subscription may be refunded in full if the request arrives within 14
days of the charge and the account has not exceeded the plan's included usage by
more than 20%. The refund is issued to the original payment method.

## 2. Renewals

Renewal charges are not refunded once the term has started, except where the
Service was unavailable for a material part of the term and the customer reports
it within 30 days.

## 3. Annual plans

An annual plan cancelled mid-term is not prorated by default. A prorated refund
may be granted at the provider's discretion when the customer cancels for a
reason attributable to the Service.

## 4. Abuse and chargebacks

Accounts with a chargeback are suspended until the dispute closes. Refunds are
not used to settle a chargeback; the processor's dispute process applies.

## 5. Statutory rights

Nothing here limits a consumer right that cannot be waived by contract. Where
local law grants a longer withdrawal period, that period applies.

## 6. How to request

Write to billing@aegislatent.com from the account email with the invoice number.
We answer within five business days.
