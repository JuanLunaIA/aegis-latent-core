A gateway for AI traffic that writes every request to an append-only ledger, and a pack the buyer can verify without trusting us.
01 · Request

A key names a tenant, a quota and a route. The gateway enforces all three before the upstream call.

02 · Ledger

The request and the response digest enter an append-only log; each entry carries the previous hash.

03 · Proof

The customer exports a signed pack and verifies inclusion offline. Our credentials are not part of the proof.

Why now

AI calls move regulated data, but the evidence is still a vendor log line. Buyers who answer to auditors need proofs they can check offline, not a dashboard that asks to be believed.

What exists today

A gateway with keys, quotas and routing; an append-only ledger with Merkle inclusion proofs; a signed export and a verifier that runs on the customer's machine. Self-serve billing, thirteen languages, one API.
